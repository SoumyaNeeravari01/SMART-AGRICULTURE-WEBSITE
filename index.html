🌱 Introduction to Smart Farming

Smart farming is the use of modern technology to improve agricultural production. It uses sensors, drones, artificial intelligence, GPS, weather information and smart irrigation systems to help farmers make better decisions.

💧 Save Water

Smart irrigation systems provide water according to the needs of crops.

🌾 Better Production

Technology helps farmers improve crop productivity.

📊 Smart Decisions

Data helps farmers understand soil, weather and crops.

👨‍🌾 Farmer Tips

  • 🌱 Select crops suitable for your soil and climate.
  • 💧 Use water efficiently and avoid unnecessary irrigation.
  • 🌦️ Check weather forecasts before important farming activities.
  • 🔬 Test soil before applying fertilizers.
  • 🐛 Regularly check crops for pests and diseases.
  • 📱 Use modern technology to monitor your farm.
  • ♻️ Follow sustainable farming practices.
